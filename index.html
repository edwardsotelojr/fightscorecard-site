Frequently asked questions

How do I score a round?

Tap a round, or tap Score round. Pick a quick score such as 10–9, or use − and + to set each fighter's score from 0 to 10. Include any point deductions in the score, then tap Save round.

Can I change a round I already scored?

Yes. Tap the round to edit it, or tap Clear round to remove its score. Totals update automatically.

How do I start a new fight?

Tap + and enter the fight title, date, fighter names and number of rounds (1–12). You can also choose a color for each corner. If your current fight has scores, it's saved to your collection first.

Where are my saved scorecards?

Tap the tray icon at the top of the screen. Tap a scorecard to keep scoring it, or swipe left on it to delete it.

How do I switch to landscape?

Tap the rotate button next to the fight title, or turn your device. On iPad, if the app is in a resizable window, open it full screen and try again.

Can I share my scorecard?

Yes. Tap the share button to send your round-by-round scores and totals as text.

How do I remove ads?

Tap Remove ads above any banner, or open Saved scorecards (the tray icon), tap the ⋯ menu, and choose Remove Ads. It's a one-time purchase.

I already bought Remove Ads. Why do I see ads?

Open Saved scorecards, tap the ⋯ menu, and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

To change whether apps can track you, go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, you can also open Saved scorecards, tap the ⋯ menu, and choose Ad Privacy Settings.

Are my scorecards backed up or synced?

Scorecards are stored only on your device. They are not synced between devices. Deleting the app deletes your scorecards. They're included in your regular iPhone or iPad backup.

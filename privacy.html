Privacy Policy

Fight Scorecard · Effective October 16, 2026

In short: Fight Scorecard does not collect, store or share any of your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. Fight Scorecard does not require an account and does not collect personal information, usage data, location, contacts, device identifiers or any other data.

Information you enter

The fight titles, dates, fighter names, corner colors and scores you enter are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you use your device's iCloud or computer backup, this data may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.

Sharing

If you tap the share button, the app gives a text copy of your scorecard to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes, such as Messages or Notes. We don't receive a copy.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Fight Scorecard contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that tracks you. It does not connect to the internet.

Deleting your data

Swipe left on a saved scorecard to delete it, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

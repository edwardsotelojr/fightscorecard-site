Privacy Policy

Fight Scorecard · Effective October 16, 2026

In short: Your scorecards stay on your device and are never sent to us. Fight Scorecard is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device and usage information to show and measure those ads. You can limit this, or buy Remove Ads to turn ads off.

Your scorecards

The fight titles, dates, fighter names, corner colors and scores you enter are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else. If you use your device's iCloud or computer backup, this data may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.

Advertising

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show banner and full-screen ads. To deliver, measure and prevent fraud in those ads, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking
  • Your IP address and the approximate location it indicates
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the ads SDK

We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: the app asks for permission before your advertising identifier can be used for personalized ads. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track," you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized using that identifier. You can change this anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: you'll be asked for consent through Google's consent form. To change your choice later, open Saved scorecards (the tray icon), tap the ⋯ menu, and choose Ad Privacy Settings.
  • Remove Ads: a one-time in-app purchase turns off all ads. Once ads are off, the app stops requesting ads from Google.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether Remove Ads has been bought on your Apple Account.

Sharing

If you tap the share button, the app gives a text copy of your scorecard to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes, such as Messages or Notes. We don't receive a copy.

Deleting your data

Swipe left on a saved scorecard to delete it, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device. To reset your advertising identifier, use your device's privacy settings.

Children

Fight Scorecard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
